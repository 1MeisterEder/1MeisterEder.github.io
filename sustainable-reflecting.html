Sustainable Reflecting

A mobile sculpture made from reflective discarded materials becomes visible through the effort of its visitors. The work asks how physical reflection can open psychological reflection.

The sculpture lights up when the visitor becomes part of its energy system.

Sustainable Reflecting connects a physical action with an inner process. Visitors generate electricity through their own movement. This energy activates a mobile sculpture built from reflective waste. Light moves through discarded material and returns to the visitor as a changing field of reflection.

The encounter is simple, but it carries a question. Does an artwork feel different when people have to contribute something with their own body before it becomes visible?

Visitor experience

From effort to light, from light to thought.

  1. 01

    The visitor enters the installation and meets an object made from reflective discarded material.

  2. 02

    Through bodily effort, the visitor generates the electricity that activates the sculpture.

  3. 03

    The sculpture begins to move, shimmer, and reflect light back into the space.

  4. 04

    A guided moment of reflection invites the visitor to think about habits, resources, relationships, and the future.

Research idea

Same sculpture
Different energy source
Meaningful participation
Research proposal to follow

The artwork stays the same. The source of energy changes.

The proposed experiment compares two versions of the same aesthetic experience. In one condition, visitors generate the electricity that activates the mobile. In another condition, visitors see the same movement and light, but the energy comes from an external source.

This makes it possible to ask whether personal contribution changes attention, meaning, memory, and willingness to reflect. The work does not try to moralise sustainability. It creates a situation where effort becomes visible and reflection becomes embodied.
