Can a contradiction interrupt habitual perception?

The project explores whether paradoxical civic statements capture more attention and sustained engagement than familiar affirmative messages. It connects sculptural public intervention with research in political psychology, attention, and communication.

A field experiment observes small moments of public engagement. People look, slow down, stop, photograph, discuss, and interact. A complementary EEG study investigates when paradoxical meaning affects attention, semantic processing, and later reflection.
