Can paradoxical framing interrupt habitual processing?

The project tests whether paradoxical civic statements capture more attention and sustained engagement than matched affirmative messages. Small sculptural figures function as a controlled presentation format for studying public attention, anthropomorphic cueing, and communication.

A field experiment measures observable micro engagement, including looking, slowing down, stopping, discussing, or interacting. A complementary EEG study examines the temporal dynamics of attentional allocation, semantic processing, and sustained elaboration.
