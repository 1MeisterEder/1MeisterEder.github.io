Eddy Eder

A scientific artivist based in Berlin, bringing psychological research and artistic practice together.

His projects transform discarded materials into figures, miniature utopias, mobile sculptures, and public interventions. Through WaMü and his independent artistic research, he asks how aesthetic experiences can alter attention, invite participation, and help people imagine shared futures.

His current studies are proposals in development. They are presented openly to invite research partners, institutional collaboration, and critical exchange.
